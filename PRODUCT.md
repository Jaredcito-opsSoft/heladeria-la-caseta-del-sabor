# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js, chosen for this first hero and the planned landing.

## Product Purpose
Present La caseta del sabor, create appetite for its raspados and help visitors view flavors, order by WhatsApp and find the shop.

## Operating Context
Physical raspadería in Tuxtla Gutiérrez, Chiapas. Public WhatsApp for orders: 961 229 87 65. Address: Calle Central Norte #1036, colonia Albania Alta, between avenida 20 de Noviembre and Monte Cristo.

## Brand Commitments
Name: La caseta del sabor. Slogan: “El raspado es primero”. Mexican, joyful, artisanal, fresh, appetizing and contemporary. The final identity, colors, logo and typography are pending from the owner.

## Evidence on Hand
Confirmed flavors: gourmet (leche quemada, pistache, nuez) and clásicos (fresa, mango, maracuyá, coco, chamoyadas, gloria, vainilla, tamarindo). No confirmed prices, hours, promotions or real product photographs were supplied. Current product images are generated concepts, not photos of items served at the shop.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This delivery covers only the animated intro and navigable hero. The full landing is a later phase. Intro target: 4–6 seconds, first visit only, optional skip, no sound, mobile friendly and reduced motion support.
